## 台帳

C01 | バイソシエーションは Koestler の造語で、1つの「面」の上で働く日常的な思考技能と、つねに複数の面で働く創造行為とを区別するために作られた | S1 p.35–36 | "I have coined the term 'bisociation' in order to make a distinction between the routine skills of thinking on a single 'plane', as it were, and the creative act, which, as I shall try to show, always operates on more than one plane."

C02 | バイソシエーションの型は、1つの状況や考え L を、それぞれ内部では筋が通っているのに普段は両立しない2つの参照枠 M1・M2 で捉えることである。L は1つの連想の文脈に結びつくのではなく、2つにバイソシエートされる | S1 p.35 ／ S7 p.17 | "The pattern underlying both stories is the perceiving of a situation or idea, L, in two self-consistent but habitually incompatible frames of reference, M 1 and M 2. The event L, in which the two intersect, is made to vibrate simultaneously on two different wavelengths, as it were. While this unusual situation lasts, L is not merely linked to one associative context, but bisociated with two."

C03 | Koestler は「参照枠」「連想の文脈」「論理の型」「行動の規範」「言説の世界」と呼んできたものを「マトリックス（matrices of thought）」という語に統一した | S1 p.38 | "I have variously referred to the two planes in Figs. 2 and 3 as 'frames of reference', 'associative contexts', 'types of logic', 'codes of behaviour', and 'universes of discourse'. Henceforth I shall use the expression 'matrices of thought' ... as a unifying formula."

C04 | Koestler はマトリックスを「固定した規則の体系に支配された、あらゆる能力・習慣・技能、秩序だった行動のパターン」と定義した | S1 p.38 ／ S2 | "I shall use the word 'matrix' to denote any ability, habit, or skill, any pattern of ordered behaviour governed by a 'code of fixed rules."

C05 | バイソシエーションという語は、創造行為で接触させられるマトリックスが独立し自律していることを指す。連想的な思考は、既存の1つのマトリックスの成員の間で働く | S1 | "The term 'bisociation' is meant to point to the independent, autonomous character of the matrices which are brought into contact in the creative act, whereas associative thought operates among members of a single pre-existing matrix."

C06 | Koestler は巻頭の三連画で、創造の3領域をユーモア・発見・芸術とし、境目ははっきり分かれず互いに溶け合うと書いた | S1 p.27 | "The three panels of the rounded triptych shown on the frontispiece indicate three domains of creativity which shade into each other without sharp boundaries: Humour, Discovery, and Art."

C07 | 創造の論理のパターンは3領域で同じで、隠れた類似を見つけることにある。違うのは感情の色合いで、滑稽な比喩には攻撃性があり、科学者の類推は感情的に中立、詩のイメージは共感的か賛嘆を伴う | S1 p.27 ／ S7 p.17 | "The logical pattern of the creative process is the same in all three cases; it consists in the discovery of hidden similarities. But the emotional climate is different in the three panels: the comic simile has a touch of aggressiveness; the scientist's reasoning by analogy is emotionally detached, i.e. neutral; the poetic image is sympathetic or admiring, inspired by a positive kind of emotion."

C08 | 2つの独立したマトリックスが相互作用した結果は、笑いに終わる衝突か、新しい知的総合への融合か、美的経験としての対置のいずれかになる。同じ1組のマトリックスが、滑稽にも悲劇的にも知的な挑戦にもなりうる | S1 p.45 | "When two independent matrices of perception or reasoning interact with each other the result (as I hope to show) is either a collision ending in laughter, or their fusion in a new intellectual synthesis, or their confrontation in an aesthetic experience. The bisociative patterns found in any domain of creative activity are tri-valent: that is to say, the same pair of matrices can produce comic, tragic, or intellectually challenging effects."

C09 | 笑いはマトリックスの衝突で生じ、発見はその統合で、美的経験はその並置で生じる | S1 | "We have seen how laughter is sparked off by the collision of matrices; discovery, by their integration; aesthetic experience by their juxtaposition."

C10 | ドン・キホーテは、置かれる三連画の面によって滑稽な人物にも悲劇的な人物にも初期の妄想の症例にもなる。3つの場合のいずれでも、現実と妄想のマトリックス（風車と幻の騎士）が読者の心の中で向き合う。第1の場合は衝突して悪意が笑いとしてこぼれ、第2の場合は2つの世界が並置されたままで共感が保たれ、第3の場合は2つが総合に融け合って臨床家の診断になる | S1 p.304–305 | "Don Quixote is a comic or a tragic figure, or a case-history of incipient paranoia, depending on the panel of the tryptich in which lie is placed. In all three cases the matrices of reality and delusion — of windmills and phantom-knights — confront each other in the reader's mini In the first case they collide, and malice is spilled in laughter. In the second, the two universes remain juxtaposed, reason osculates to and fro between them, compassion remains attached to it ... In the third case, the two merge in a synthesis: the (emotionally neutral') diagnosis of the clinician."

C11 | Koestler はバイソシエーションの型を示す詳しい例として、グーテンベルクの活版印刷の発明、ケプラーによる天文学と物理学の総合、ダーウィンの自然選択による進化論の3つを挙げた | S1 p.121 | "I shall give three somewhat more detailed examples which display this pattern from various angles: Gutenberg's invention of printing with movable types; Kepler's synthesis of astronomy and physics; Darwin's theory of evolution by natural selection."

C12 | Koestler がグーテンベルクの経緯の根拠にしたのは、Frère Cordelier 宛の一連の書簡である。Koestler はこれを「本物らしい響きがあり、発明に至った様子を生き生きと描いている」と書いている | S1 p.122 | "But there exists a series of letters to a correspondent, Frere Cordelier, which has an authentic ring and gives a graphic description of the manner in which Gutenberg arrived at his invention."

C13 | 書簡でグーテンベルクは、トランプ札や聖人画が小さな木片に彫られ、濃いインクを塗って少し湿った紙を載せ、紙の裏が磨かれるまで擦ると絵や文字が写ると述べている | S1 p.122 | "These cards and pictures are engraved on small pieces of wood, and below the pictures there are words and entire lines also engraved A thick ink is applied to the engraving; and upon this a leaf of paper, slightly damp, is placed; then this wood, this ink, this paper is rubbed and rubbed until the back of the paper is polished."

C14 | 書簡でグーテンベルクは、数行でできたことを本全体、まず聖書でやりたいと述べ、1300ページ全部を木に彫ることを考えるのは無駄だと書いている | S1 p.122–123 | "Well, what has been done for a few words, for a few lines, I must succeed in doing for large pages of writing, ... for whole books, for the first of all books, the Bible. . . . How? It is useless to think of engraving on pieces of wood the whole thirteen hundred pages. . . ."

C15 | Koestler は、木版を擦って刷る方法をグーテンベルクのマトリックス（技能）第1号と名づけた | S1 p.123 | "Here, then, we have matrix or skill No. i: the printing from wood-blocks by means of rubbing."

C16 | 硬貨の打刻からの類推で、グーテンベルクは印章に行き着いた。書簡では、共同体の印章を羊皮紙や紙に押せばすべてが済む、印章は必要なだけ何度でも繰り返せると述べている | S1 p.123 | "It leads Gutenberg, by way of analogy, to the seal: 'When you apply to the vellum or paper the seal of your community, everything has been said, everything is done, everything is there. Do you not see that you can repeat as many times as necessary the seal covered with signs and characters?'"

C17 | 硬貨や印章の形に文字を鋳造しても、擦るという不器用な方法ではきれいな印は出ない。探索が「擦る」という唯一の伝統的な方法に閉じ込められている限り、問題は行き詰まったままだった。解くにはまったく別の種類の技能を持ち込む必要があった。あれこれ試す期間は「孵化（incubation）」の期間だった | S1 p.123 | "Yet all this is insufficient. He may cast letters in the form of coins, or seals, instead of engraving the wood, yet they will never make a clear print by the clumsy rubbing method; so long as his search remains confined to this one and only traditional method of making an 'imprint', the problem remains blocked. To solve it, an entirely different kind of skill must be brought in. He tries this and that; he thinks of everything under the sun: it is the period of incubation."

C18 | 書簡でグーテンベルクは、ぶどうの収穫に加わってワインが流れるのを見、結果から原因へさかのぼって、何ものも抗えないこの圧搾機の力を調べたと書いている | S1 p.123 | "I took part in the wine harvest. I watched the wine flowing, and going back from the effect to the cause, I studied the power of this press which nothing can resist. . . ."

C19 | その「ひらめきの光」とは、ぶどう圧搾機と印章のバイソシエーションであり、2つを足すと活版印刷機になる。ぶどう圧搾機は、果肉や流れる赤い液体や陽気な宴という文脈から持ち上げられ、羊皮紙に印章を押すことと結びつけられた | S1 p.123 | "'The ray of light' was the bisociation of wine-press and seal — which, added together, become the letter-press. The wine-press has been lifted out of its context, the mushy pulp, the flowing red liquid, the jolly revelry ... and connected with the stamping of vellum with a seal."

C20 | それ以前は肉屋とパン屋と燭台屋ほどに違っていた別々の技能が、これ以後は1つの複雑なマトリックスに統合されて現れる | S1 p.124 | "From now onward these separate skills, which previously had been as different as the butchers, the baker's, and the candlestick-maker's, will appear integrated in a single, complex matrix"

C21 | 状況が行き詰まったときは、まっすぐな思考に代えて「脇に考える（thinking aside）」、つまり行き詰まりを解く新しい補助のマトリックスを探す必要がある。発見の本質はそのマトリックスに行き当たることで、グーテンベルクはぶどう圧搾機に、ケプラーは太陽の力に行き当たった | S1 p.163–164 | "When a situation is blocked, straight thinking must be superseded by 'thinking aside' — the search for a new, auxiliary matrix which will unblock it, without having ever before been called to perform such a task. The essence of discovery is to hit upon such a matrix — as Gutenberg hit on the wine-press and Kepler on the sun-force."

C22 | 駄じゃれは、1つの音の形を2つの意味にバイソシエートしたもので、2本の思考の糸を音の結び目で縛ったものである | S1 p.64–65（S7 p.17 が引用） | "The pun is the bisociation of a single phonetic form with two meanings – two strings of thought tied together by an acoustic knot"

C23 | Arthur Koestler はオーストリア＝ハンガリー生まれの作家・ジャーナリストで、1905年9月5日ブダペスト生まれ、1983年3月1日ロンドン没である | S3 | "Austro-Hungarian-born author and journalist" ／ born September 5, 1905, in Budapest; died March 1, 1983, in London

C25 | Fauconnier と Turner は、Koestler の1964年の著作を概念融合（conceptual blending）の重要な先行研究として挙げた。Koestler が「マトリックスのバイソシエーション」と呼んだ創造の型を土台にしている | S4 | "Turner and Fauconnier identified Arthur Koestler's 1964 work The Act of Creation as an important precursor. Koestler had identified a pattern in creative accomplishments that he termed 'bisociation of matrices', which the blending theorists built upon as foundational to their framework."

C26 | 概念融合では、異なる場面の要素と関係が無意識の過程で混ぜ合わされるとされ、この過程は日常の思考と言語に遍在すると仮定されている。基本のモデルは、総称空間・2つの入力空間・融合空間の4つからなる | S4 | "Elements and vital relations from diverse scenarios are 'blended' in a subconscious process, which is assumed to be ubiquitous to everyday thought and language." ／ "a generic space (capturing common structure), input spaces (providing specific contents), a blended space (integrating selected elements), and cross-space mappings"

C27 | 1つの領域の中の概念の関係を表す連想に対し、バイソシエーションは（たいてい間接的な）つながりを見つけて複数領域の情報を融合させる | S5 p.200 | "In contrast to an association representing a relation between concepts within one domain, a bisociation fuses the information in multiple domains by finding a (usually indirect) connection between them"

C28 | BisoNets の文脈での形式的な定義は「バイソシエーションとは、領域を定義する特定の見方によっては互いにつながっていない、2つ以上の領域の概念を結ぶリンクである」 | S5 p.202 | "A bisociation is a link that connects concepts from two or more domains, which are unconnected depending on the specific view by which the domains are defined."

C29 | バイソシエーションの型として、橋渡しになる概念・橋渡しになるグラフ・構造の類似の3つが論じられている | S5 p.203 | "We have discussed three di erent notions of bisociation: bridging concepts, bridging graphs and structural similarity."

C30 | Poincaré は、選ばれた組み合わせのうち最も実り多いものは、しばしば遠く離れた領域から引かれた要素でできていると述べた | S5 p.200 | "Among chosen combinations the most fertile will often be those formed of elements drawn from domains which are far apart... C31 | Ahmed と Fuge は、社会課題のアイデア投稿コミュニティ OpenIDEO の数千件のアイデアを使い、領域をまたぐ着想源を見つける計算の枠組みを提案した | S6 Abstract | "Using a dataset of thousands of ideas from OpenIDEO, an online collaborative community, our results show usefulness of representing conceptual bridges through collections of words (topics) in finding crossdomain inspiration."

C32 | 例に使われた3つのテーマは、都市環境における女性の安全の改善、不法な摘発のリスクの低減、経済が衰退する都市の活気の向上である。表面上はかなり似ていない領域だが、3つのテーマにまたがる複数のアイデアが「身につける装具を使う」という考えを用いていた | S6 §1.2 | "each of the three challenges—improving womens' safety in urban environments, reducing the risk of unlawful detection, and increasing vibrancy in cites facing economic decline—had several ideas ...
