# 出典台帳：デザイン思考

C01 | デザイン思考は、デザイナーの感性と手法を使い、人のニーズを、技術的に実現できることと、事業戦略が顧客価値と市場機会に変えられることに合わせる分野である | Tim Brown, "Design Thinking", Harvard Business Review, June 2008（https://hbr.org/2008/06/design-thinking 、本文PDF: https://readings.design/PDF/Tim%20Brown,%20Design%20Thinking.pdf） | "it is a discipline that uses the designer's sensibility and methods to match people's needs with what is technologically feasible and what a viable business strategy can convert into customer value and market opportunity."
C02 | 歴史的にデザインは開発工程の下流の段階として扱われてきた。革新の実質的な作業に先立って関わっていないデザイナーが、あとからやってきて考えに美しい包装をかける地点だった | Brown 2008 | "Historically, design has been treated as a downstream step in the development process—the point where designers, who have played no earlier role in the substantive work of innovation, come along and put a beautiful wrapper around the idea."
C03 | いまは、できあがった考えを消費者にとって魅力的にするようデザイナーに頼むのではなく、消費者のニーズと欲求により良く応える考えそのものを作ることを企業が求めている。前者の役割は戦術的で、生み出す価値は限られる | Brown 2008 | "Now, however, rather than asking designers to make an already developed idea more attractive to consumers, companies are asking them to create ideas that better meet consumers' needs and desires. The former role is tactical, and results in limited value creation"
C04 | デザインの工程は、あらかじめ決められた順序立った手順ではなく、空間の体系として表すのがいちばんよい | Brown 2008 | "The design process is best described metaphorically as a system of spaces rather than a predefined series of orderly steps."
C05 | デザインの企画は3つの空間を通る。解決を探させる状況（問題・機会・その両方）を指す「inspiration」、解決につながりうる案を生み・育て・試す過程を指す「ideation」、市場への道筋を描くことを指す「implementation」。とくに最初の2つは、案が練られ新しい方向が取られるにつれ何度も行き来する | Brown 2008 | "Design projects must ultimately pass through three spaces ... We label these \"inspiration,\" for the circumstances (be they a problem, an opportunity, or both) that motivate the search for solutions; \"ideation,\" for the process of generating, developing, and testing ideas that may lead to solutions; and \"implementation,\" for the charting of a path to market. Projects will loop back through these spaces—particularly the first two—more than once as ideas are refined and new directions taken."
C06 | 2004年、自転車部品メーカーの島野（Shimano）は、米国の高級ロードレース車とマウンテンバイクの分野で成長が頭打ちになっていた。同社はつねに技術の革新で成長してきたため、次の革新がどこから来るかを当然のように予測しようとし、今回は団塊世代に受ける高級カジュアル車が面白い領域になると考えた。IDEOが協働に招かれた | Brown 2008 | "In 2004 Shimano, a Japanese manufacturer of bicycle components, faced flattening growth in its traditional high-end road-racing and mountain-bike segments in the United States. The company had always relied on technology innovations to drive its growth and naturally tried to predict where the next one might come from. This time Shimano thought a high-end casual bike that appealed to boomers would be an interesting area to explore. IDEO was invited to collaborate on the project."
C07 | inspirationの段階で、IDEOと島野のデザイナー・行動科学者・マーケター・エンジニアからなる分野をまたいだチームが、企画にふさわしい制約を見極める作業をした。チームは高級市場より広く見るべきだという勘から出発し、アメリカの成人の90%が自転車に乗らない理由を調べることにした | Brown 2008 | "During the inspiration phase, an interdisciplinary team of IDEO and Shimano people—designers, behavioral scientists, marketers, and engineers—worked to identify appropriate constraints for the project. The team began with a hunch that it should focus more broadly than on the high-end market, which might prove to be neither the only nor even the best source of new growth. So it set out to learn why 90% of American adults don't ride bikes."
C08 | 会ったほとんどの人が子どもの頃に自転車に乗り、楽しい記憶を持っていた。一方で多くのアメリカ人がいまの自転車に気後れしていた。理由は、店での体験（多くの独立系自転車店で接客する、ぴったりした競技服の若い選手風の店員を含む）、自転車・付属品・専用衣服の複雑さと値段、自転車向けに作られていない道路を走る危なさ、たまにしか乗らないのに技術的に複雑な自転車の整備が要ること | Brown 2008 | "They discovered that nearly everyone they met rode a bike as a child and had happy memories of doing so. They also discovered that many Americans are intimidated by cycling today—by the retail experience (including the young, Lycra-clad athletes who serve as sales staff in most independent bike stores); by the complexity and cost of the bikes, accessories, and specialized clothing; by the danger of cycling on roads not designed for bicycles; and by the demands of maintaining a technically sophisticated bike that is ridden infrequently."
C09 | 島野の中心顧客の外にいる人から得たこの人間中心の探索が、自転車の新しい分野をまるごと作ればアメリカの消費者を子どもの頃の体験に結び直し、気後れの根本の原因にも手を打てるという気づきにつながり、大きな未開拓の市場が見えた | Brown 2008 | "This human-centered exploration—which took its insights from people outside Shimano's core customer base—led to the realization that a whole new category of bicycling might be able to reconnect American consumers to their experiences as children while also dealing with the root causes of their feelings of intimidation—thus revealing a large untapped market."
C10 | デザインチームは「コースティング（Coasting）」という考えに行き着いた。自転車から離れた人を、単純で分かりやすく楽しい活動に誘うことを狙った。自転車は速度が上がったり落ちたりすると自動で変速し、座面は快適に詰め物がされ、操作しやすく、整備もあまり要らないものにした | Brown 2008 | "The design team, responsible for every aspect of what was envisioned as a holistic experience, came up with the concept of \"Coasting.\" Coasting would aim to entice lapsed bikers into an activity that was simple, straightforward, and fun. ... automatically as the bicycle gained speed or slowed. The bikes would feature comfortably padded seats, be easy to operate, and require relatively little maintenance."
C11 | トレック、ラレー、ジャイアントの大手3社が島野の部品を組み込んだ新しい自転車を開発した。チームは自転車だけで止めず、初心者が気後れしないよう独立系販売店向けの店頭の売り方を作り、コースティングを人生を楽しむ方法として示すブランドを作り、自治体や自転車団体と組んで安全に走れる場所を示す広報活動も設計した | Brown 2008 | "Three major manufacturers—Trek, Raleigh, and Giant—developed new bikes incorporating innovative components from Shimano. But the design team didn't stop with the bike itself. In-store retailing strategies were created for independent bike dealers, in part to alleviate the discomfort that biking novices felt in stores designed to serve enthusiasts. The team developed a brand that identified Coasting as a way to enjoy life. ... And it designed a public relations campaign—in collaboration with local governments and cycling organizations—that identified safe places to ride."
C12 | デザインチームが担うと誰もが思っていた唯一のもの、つまり自転車の見た目は、意図的に開発工程の後ろへ回された。2007年の発売が成功したあと、2008年にはさらに7社の自転車メーカーがコースティング車の製造に名乗りを上げた | Brown 2008 | "the single thing one would have expected the design team to be responsible for—the look of the bikes—was intentionally deferred to later in the development process, when the team created a reference design to inspire the bike companies' own design teams. After a successful launch in 2007, seven more bicycle manufacturers signed up to produce Coasting bikes in 2008."
C13 | 医療事業者のカイザー・パーマネンテは、看護師・医師・管理者にデザイン思考の手法を教えることで、現場の実務者が新しい考えを出すことを狙った。数か月にわたり、IDEOとカイザーのコーチの助けを得た研修が行われた | Brown 2008 | "By teaching design thinking techniques to nurses, doctors, and administrators, Kaiser hoped to inspire its practitioners to contribute new ideas. Over the course of several months Kaiser teams participated in workshops with the help of my firm, IDEO, and a group of Kaiser coaches."
C14 | 4つの病院で看護師の勤務交代をやり直す企画の中心チームには、元看護師の戦略担当、組織開発の専門家、技術の専門家、工程の設計者、労働組合の代表、IDEOのデザイナーが入った。このチームが各病院の現場の実務者チームと組んで動いた | Brown 2008 | "The core project team included a strategist (formerly a nurse), an organizational-development specialist, a technology expert, a process designer, a union representative, and designers from IDEO. This group worked with innovation teams of frontline practitioners in each of the four hospitals."
C15 | 看護師は毎回の勤務の最初の45分を、ナースステーションで前の勤務者から患者の状態を引き継ぐことに使っていた。引き継ぎの方法は病院ごとに違い、録音の口述から対面の会話まであった。時間をかけているのに、患者が前の勤務時間をどう過ごしたか、どの家族が付き添っていたか、検査や治療が行われたかなど、患者にとって最も大事なことが伝わらないことが多かった | Brown 2008 | "nurses routinely spent the first 45 minutes of each shift at the nurses' station debriefing the departing shift about the status of patients. Their methods of information exchange were different in every hospital, ranging from recorded dictation to face-to-face conversations. ... Despite a significant investment of time, the nurses often failed to learn some of the things that mattered most to patients, such as how they had fared during the previous shift, which family members were with them, and whether or not certain tests or therapies had been administered."
C16 | カイザーの看護チームが成し遂げたことは、突然のひらめきでも天才のきらめきでもなく、人間中心の発見の過程に支えられた地道な作業と、それに続く試作・検証・改良の繰り返しの結果だった | Brown 2008 | "what the Kaiser nursing team accomplished was neither a sudden breakthrough nor the lightning strike of genius; it was the result of hard work augmented by a creative human-centered discovery process and followed by iterative cycles of prototyping, testing, and refinement."
C17 | Tim BrownはIDEOのCEO兼社長で、IDEOはカリフォルニア州パロアルトに本社を置く革新とデザインの会社である | Brown 2008 | "Tim Brown (tbrown@ideo.com) is the CEO and president of IDEO, an innovation and design firm with headquarters in Palo Alto, California."
C18 | デザイン思考は、Fasteのスタンフォードでの同僚だったDavid M. Kelleyによって事業向けに取り入れられた。Kelleyは1991年にIDEOを創業した | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_thinking | "Design thinking was adapted for business purposes by Faste's Stanford colleague David M. Kelley, who founded IDEO in 1991."
C19 | Rolf Fasteは1980年代から1990年代にかけてスタンフォード大学でMcKimの仕事を発展させ、創造的な行動の方法としてデザイン思考を教えた | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_thinking | "Rolf Faste expanded on McKim's work at Stanford University in the 1980s and 1990s, teaching design thinking as a method of creative action."
C20 | Peter G. Roweの1987年の著書『Design Thinking』は、デザイン研究の文献におけるこの言葉の重要な初期の用例にあたる | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_thinking | "Peter G. Rowe's 1987 book Design Thinking provided significant early academic usage of the term."
C21 | スタンフォードのHasso Plattner Institute of Design（d.school）は、広く応用できる革新の方法として2003年にデザイン思考を教え始めた | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_thinking | "The Hasso Plattner Institute of Design (d.school) at Stanford began teaching design thinking in 2003 as a generalizable approach to innovation."
C22 | 批判する側は、デザイン思考の短期的で目新しく素朴な案への偏りが、現実的でなく足場のない提案を生んできたと論じている | Rebecca Ackermann, "Design thinking was supposed to fix the world. Where did it go wrong?", MIT Technology Review, 2023-02-09 | "Critics have argued that its short-term focus on novel and naive ideas has resulted in unrealistic and ungrounded recommendations."
C23 | サンフランシスコの学校給食では、IDEOが2013年に関わった数年後の分析で、在籍者が増え続けているのに、給食を選ぶ子どもの数は同じか、むしろ減っていた | Ackermann 2023 | "An analysis a few years after IDEO's 2013 engagement showed that about the same number of kids or even fewer were choosing to eat school lunch, despite a continuous increase in enrollment."
C24 | ザンビアのルサカで行われたDiva Centresの企画は、2016年にCore77のサービスデザイン賞を受けたが、資金と提供体制が入り組んでいて、3か所の模範施設を超えて広がらなかった | Ackermann 2023 | "the Diva Centres project in Lusaka, Zambia, where IDEO's design thinking approach produced a pilot that 'won a Core77 Service Design Award in 2016' but struggled to scale beyond three model sites due to complex funding and service delivery issues."
C25 | 最初の段階が「共感」であるにもかかわらず、実務者はほとんど共感をしていない。使う人は情報を出す役にとどめられ、聞き取りや観察をする側は使う人と同じ生活の経験や背景を持たないことが多い。デザイン思考の場を率いるのはたいてい外部のコンサルタントである | Ackermann 2023 | "even though its first step is 'empathize,' practitioners do little of it ... Users are mostly relegated to being info providers, and the interviewers and observers often have no lived experience or background that matches the users', with those leading design thinking processes usually being external consultants who are well-educated, Western, and White."
