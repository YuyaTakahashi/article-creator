# 出典台帳：ソフト・ラベリング

C01 | 大きなモデルは正解以外の分類にも確率を割り当てており、その中には他よりずっと大きいものがある | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "a side-effect of the learning is that the trained model assigns probabilities to all of the incorrect answers and even when these probabilities are very small, some of them are much larger than others."
C02 | 不正解の確率の相対的な大きさは、そのモデルがどう一般化するかを多く物語る。BMWの画像がゴミ収集車と間違えられる見込みは小さいが、ニンジンと間違えられるよりは何倍も大きい | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "The relative probabilities of incorrect answers tell us a lot about how the cumbersome model tends to generalize. An image of a BMW, for example, may only have a very small chance of being mistaken for a garbage truck, but that mistake is still many times more probable than mistaking it for a carrot."
C03 | 大きなモデルが出す分類の確率を、小さなモデルを学習させるための「ソフトターゲット」として使う | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "An obvious way to transfer the generalization ability of the cumbersome model to a small model is to use the class probabilities produced by the cumbersome model as \"soft targets\" for training the small model."
C04 | ソフトターゲットのエントロピーが高いとき、ハードターゲットより1件あたり多くの情報を与え、学習の勾配のばらつきも小さいので、小さなモデルはずっと少ないデータで学習できる | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "When the soft targets have high entropy, they provide much more information per training case than hard targets and much less variance in the gradient between training cases, so the small model can often be trained on much less data than the original cumbersome model and using a much higher learning rate."
C05 | MNISTのように大きなモデルがほぼ確実に正解する課題では、学習した関数の情報の多くがソフトターゲットのごく小さな確率どうしの比に入っている。ある「2」は3である確率が10のマイナス6乗、7である確率が10のマイナス9乗で、別の「2」では逆になる | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "much of the information about the learned function resides in the ratios of very small probabilities in the soft targets. For example, one version of a 2 may be given a probability of 10−6 of being a 3 and 10−9 of being a 7 whereas for another version it may be the other way around. This is valuable information that defines a rich similarity structure over the data (i.e. it says which 2's look like 3's and which look like 7's)"
C06 | ソフトマックスの温度Tを上げると、分類の確率分布がやわらかくなる。Tは通常1である | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "where T is a temperature that is normally set to 1. Using a higher value for T produces a softer probability distribution over classes."
C07 | MNISTで、1200ユニット2層の大きなネットは67個のテスト誤り、正則化なしの800ユニット2層の小さなネットは146個の誤りだった。小さなネットに、温度20のソフトターゲットに合わせる課題を足すと74個の誤りになった | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "This net achieved 67 test errors whereas a smaller net with two hidden layers of 800 rectified linear hidden units and no regularization achieved 146 errors. But if the smaller net was regularized solely by adding the additional task of matching the soft targets produced by the large net at a temperature of 20, it achieved 74 test errors."
C08 | 転移セットから数字の3をすべて除いても、蒸留したモデルはバイアスを3.5上げると109個の誤りで、そのうち3は14個だった。3を一度も見ずにテストの3の98.6%を正しく当てた | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "We then tried omitting all examples of the digit 3 from the transfer set. ... If this bias is increased by 3.5 (which optimizes overall performance on the test set), the distilled model makes 109 errors of which 14 are on 3s. So with the right bias, the distilled model gets 98.6% of the test 3s correct despite never having seen a 3 during training."
C09 | 大きなモデル全体で予測するのは扱いにくく、多数のユーザーへ届けるには計算量が大きすぎることがある | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "making predictions using a whole ensemble of models is cumbersome and may be too computationally expensive to allow deployment to a large number of users, especially if the individual models are large neural nets."
C10 | 著者はGeoffrey Hinton、Oriol Vinyals、Jeff Deanの3名で、いずれもGoogle所属。2015年3月9日にarXivへ投稿された | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "Geoffrey Hinton, Google Inc. / Oriol Vinyals, Google Inc. / Jeff Dean, Google Inc." / "arXiv:1503.02531v1 [stat.ML] 9 Mar 2015"
C11 | 通常の単一ラベル分類では、注釈者は1件に1つのラベルだけを付け、多数決で最終的なハードラベルを決めるときに注釈者どうしの不一致は捨てられる | https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.355.pdf | "Typically, in such tasks annotators are only asked to provide a single label for each sample and annotator disagreement is discarded when a final hard label is decided through majority voting."
C12 | 確信度・第2候補のラベル・不一致といった注釈者の追加情報からソフトラベルを作れる。これで分類器を学習させると、ハードラベルのテストセットでの性能と較正が良くなる | https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.355.pdf | "Our findings indicate that additional annotator information, such as confidence, secondary label and disagreement, can be used to effectively generate soft labels. Training classifiers with these soft labels then leads to improved performance and calibration on the hard label test set."
C13 | ハードラベルのやり方は、注釈の過程にある有用な情報を無視し、現実のデータに含まれる不確かさと込み入った事情をとらえそこなう | https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.355.pdf | "this hard label approach tends to ignore valuable information from the annotation process, failing to capture the uncertainties and intricacies in real-world data (Uma et al., 2021)."
C14 | ソフトラベルを使う手法には、知識蒸留（Hintonら2015）、ラベルスムージング（Szegedyら2016）、確信度に基づくラベル付け（Collinsら2022）、注釈の集約（Umaら2020）がある | https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.355.pdf | "An emerging alternative approach that addresses these limitations is the use of soft labels through techniques such as Knowledge Distillation (Hinton et al., 2015), Label Smoothing (Szegedy et al., 2016), Confidence-based Labeling (Collins et al., 2022), and Annotation Aggregation (Uma et al., 2020)."
C15 | 論文の著者はシェフィールド大学計算機科学科のBen Wuら6名、2023年のEMNLP Findingsに採録 | https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.355.pdf | "Don't Waste a Single Annotation: Improving Single-Label Classifiers Through Soft Labels / Ben Wu, Yue Li, Yida Mu, Carolina Scarton, Kalina Bontcheva and Xingyi Song / Department of Computer Science, The University of Sheffield, Sheffield, UK"
C16 | ソフトラベルでの学習は、分類が難しいデータほどハードラベルより少ないニューロン数で同じ損失に達する | https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09579 | "soft-label training using gradient descent requires only O(1/(γ²ε)) neurons to achieve a classification loss averaged over epochs smaller than some ε > 0" / "more pronounced as the dataset becomes increasingly difficult to classify"

C17 | ごく小さな確率どうしの比には豊かな似かたの構造が入っているが、値が0に近すぎるため、転移の段階では交差エントロピーの損失にほとんど影響しない | https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531 | "This is valuable information that defines a rich similarity structure over the data (i. e. it says which 2's look like 3's and which look like 7's) but it has very little influence on the cross-entropy cost function during the transfer stage because the probabilities are so close to zero."
